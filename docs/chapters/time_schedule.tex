\chapter{TIME SCHEDULE}%
\label{chap:time_analysis}

Time analysis is performed to estimate and manage the duration required for each phase of the 
project. Proper scheduling ensures timely completion of the project and effective utilization 
of available resources.

\section{PROJECT TIMELINE}

The development of the digital clock project was divided into multiple phases based on the 
Waterfall development model. Each phase was allocated a specific time period, as summarized in 
Gnatt Chart~\ref{fig:gantt_chart}.

The time allocation was sufficient to complete the project within the academic schedule. Minor 
adjustments were made during the testing phase to resolve timing and display-related issues.
